What two studies measured about virtual tours

A study published in 2025 in the journal Information Systems Research (Yan, Meng and Tan) analysed about 43,000 properties listed on a real-estate platform in China in 2018 and 2019. Properties with a virtual-reality tour stayed on the market 28% to 49% less time. The sale price did not change.

A study presented in 2026 at the European Real Estate Society conference (Wang and Yang) analysed 46,920 sales in Beijing from 2017 to 2021. With a virtual-reality tour, the time to sell was 26% shorter.

Both studies measured tours of listed properties in China. They did not measure development models, nor this product.

How we deliver

You send the project and whatever media you have; we build the tower, the city around it, the sun and the apartment inside. Nothing goes public before you approve it.

  1. 01You send

    Through a page with 16 slots. The mandatory part is what your approved project already has; photos and videos are optional and make the model more faithful. Each slot says what we make with the material and what we use without it. The link arrives by e-mail, with no password and no sign-up, and stays valid for 30 days.

  2. 02We build

    The tower with every unit in its place, the city around it, the sun calculation and the 360 tour. The timing is written into the proposal.

  3. 03You approve

    Before it goes live, you receive the full preview: the building in its city, every unit with its price and the tour inside. What goes public is what you approved in that preview.

    The developer or the brokerage is the advertiser: its name, licence and legal information go on the development's page.

  4. 04Link goes live

    One link for the development and one per unit, ready to send and to print as a QR code.

What it costs

A project at the level of the studio demo (1 tower, 1 unit type with the interior tour, up to 6 points) costs from R$25,000 setup + R$2,000 a month. In US dollars, about US$5,014 + US$401 a month; in euros, about EUR 4,473 + EUR 358 a month, converted at the PTAX rate of 5 October 2026.

A one-time setup per development plus a monthly fee, billed month to month. What the monthly fee covers is written into the proposal. More towers, more unit types, more points or a building not yet built: each case gets its own quote. In this preview, ask the person who sent you the link for the proposal. It goes out by e-mail.
