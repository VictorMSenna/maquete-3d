Questions

What if…

What do I send?

For the draft: address, floor plans and price list. For the full project: sections, elevations, area schedule, specification and your logo. The upload page has 16 slots and says what we do with each one.

What if the building isn't built yet?

The building comes from the approved project: floor plans, sections, elevations and specification. The interior comes from our kit, with your plan. The city and the sun are the address's own, and that case is quoted in the chat.

What if the 3D city doesn't cover the address?

We check before the proposal. If it does not, you know before paying anything.

And the deadline?

It starts when the complete material on the list arrives. If the material is late, the deadline moves day for day. Each delivery has 7 business days for your list of adjustments. No reply in that window counts as approval.

What if I cancel?

If you cancel before the full preview, the deposit stays with us. After the preview, we charge only for the work already done.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting of the link, support by WhatsApp and e-mail in business hours, with a reply within 4 business hours, and 2 price-list and availability updates a month, done by us within 1 business day.

And to end it?

No minimum term. Give 30 days' notice and the page stays live to the end of the paid month. Then we keep the files for 30 days to reactivate without redoing anything.

What if the project changes?

A new unit type, a new tower or a façade change sit outside the monthly fee and are quoted separately.

Do you shoot photos or drone footage?

No. The media is yours: photos, video and drone footage come from you. Without them, we work from the project and the 3D city.
