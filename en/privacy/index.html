Privacy notice

Preview version: the final page carries the details of the company that publishes this site.

What the chat keeps

The chat on this site asks for no login and no sign-up. We keep the text of the conversation. Your e-mail only comes in when you ask for the proposal, and it is used to send the proposal, the contract and the signature.

Voice messages

The audio is transcribed and deleted right after transcription. What remains is the text you checked and sent.

Processing by artificial intelligence

Chat messages are processed by an artificial-intelligence service with servers in the United States.

WhatsApp

If you talk to us on WhatsApp, the messages go through WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) and stay with us for the same period as the site's conversations.

Google Maps

This site includes Google Maps features and content, at the top and in the demo. When they load, your browser connects to Google's servers. Use of Google Maps features and content is subject to the then-current versions of the Google Maps End User Additional Terms of Service (https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

How long we keep it

Site conversations and WhatsApp messages stay with us for the period written on the final version of this page. After that, they are deleted.

How to request access, correction or deletion

The address for requesting access, correction or deletion is on the final version of this page.
